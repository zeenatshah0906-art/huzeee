To my favourite person, ❤️

Happy Boyfriend Day to the person who somehow became such an important part of my life. 🫶🏻

I don't think I say it enough, but I'm genuinely so grateful for you. For every little thing you do, every conversation, every stupid joke, every moment you make me smile, and even for the little things you probably don't realise mean so much to me.

I love that with you, I can just be me. I can be silly, annoying, emotional, quiet, dramatic, or completely random—and somehow, you still choose to stay. 😭❤️ You've become one of those people whose presence itself makes everything feel a little better.

And there's something I secretly pray for whenever I think about us. In Sha Allah, I hope we're not just a part of each other's lives for a little while. I hope Allah keeps us together through every phase, every difficult day, every change, and every beautiful moment that's yet to come. And if we're written for each other, I hope one day we get to make it official, build our little life together, and look back at all of this with a smile.

I don't just want memories with you—I want years of them. And maybe that's why a part of me wishes that if Allah allows it, we stay together till Qayamat, and even meet each other again in Jannat. 🤍

Thank you for being you. Thank you for making me feel loved, for listening to me, for putting up with my moods, and for giving me so many little moments that I'll probably remember for a very long time.

You may not realise it, but you've become a really beautiful part of my life. And I hope you always remember that you are loved—not just on days like Boyfriend Day, but on all the completely ordinary days too.

Happy Boyfriend Day, my love. ❤️

Here's to us, to all the memories we've already made, and to all the ones I hope are still waiting for us.

And yes… I still have a hug pending. 🫂❤️

Yours, always.
